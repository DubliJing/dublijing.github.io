About Me

I am a Master's student in Media Informatics at RWTH Aachen University. My research focuses on large language model (LLM), visual-language navigation (VLN), and LLM/VLM-based robot control.

Research Interests

  • Embodied AI & vision-language navigation (VLN)
  • LLM / VLM-based robot control and task planning
  • Multimodal learning and instruction following
  • Computer vision and semantic segmentation
